# Activity: feed first (2026-11-12)

Activity now leads with its entries. The summary is one slim strip, the filters are one row, and the days are a flat dated feed. Calculations, privacy rules, period loading, search and hand-ordering are unchanged; this is presentation.

## What changed

- **Spent strip** ([ActivitySummaryStrip](../src/features/transactions/ActivitySummaryStrip.tsx)) replaces the big Spent card. It shows the kicker ("Spent this week", "Spent on Food this week", or just "Spent" for a past period), the figure, the change against the period before (dropped while the list is narrowed to a category) and a small bar per day (per week in a month) with the current day in ink. It is a button that opens Insights. The figure is a plain amount that never counts up; the strip as a whole glides in when the period steps (see below).
- **Insights sheet** holds what the card used to: the figure and change, the full day chart (tap a day to see only it, which closes the sheet and scrolls the list), the daily average, and Money in / Left. It is the same [TransactionsHeadline](../src/features/transactions/TransactionsHeadline.tsx) with `bare`, so the chart, scaling and privacy logic are not duplicated.
- **One filter row** ([ActivityFilterChips](../src/features/transactions/ActivityFilterChips.tsx)): the Filters button (with its count), then the picked category and account chips with Clear all, then All / Spent / Income / Transfers. It scrolls sideways, and wraps at 360 dp or above 1.2 font scale. The second chip line is gone.
- **Flat dated feed** ([TimelineDay](../src/features/transactions/TimelineDay.tsx)): each day is a block on the page, not a segment of one card. A marker rail on the left shows the weekday and date number (today's in a filled circle) and a line down to the next day, which the last day ends. The day's heading names what it did instead of one unlabelled net: "₹1,420 spent · ₹82,000 in · ₹899 back" (refunds are counted as "back", not as income, and transfers move nothing). It is omitted when one line would only repeat it, and savings amounts are left out while hidden, as before.

Unchanged: the Week | Month switch and the date pill stay in the header, the entry rows (a note leads with the category, parent and account under it), stacks, drag ordering, search, the filter sheet and the detail sheet.

## After the first look on a phone

- The "avg ₹… a week" name on the dashed line was drawn before the capsules, so a capsule painted over part of it in the wider Insights sheet. The line still runs behind the capsules; its name is now drawn after them ([SpendBarChart](../src/features/transactions/SpendBarChart.tsx)).
- Opening or closing a group ("Home 2 ⌄") popped its entries in and out and made the days below jump. The entries now fade in with the shared panel motion and fade out on close, the day grows and shrinks with `ROW_LAYOUT`, and the days below slide with it. The layout animation is only on for 400 ms after a group is toggled (`animateLayout`): left on, every shift in a long windowed list (rows being measured as you scroll) would animate on every day. Reduce-motion turns all of it off, as everywhere else.
- The Spent strip lost the glide the old card made when the period stepped. [usePeriodSlide](../src/features/transactions/usePeriodSlide.ts) is that glide as one hook (18 dp from the side it came from with a fade, a plain fade for a picked period, nothing under reduce-motion); the strip and the Insights figures both use it.
- In the filter row only the four type choices form the radio group. The Filters button and the removable chips were inside it, which read wrongly to a screen reader.
- Small consistency tweaks with Home, no layout change: the type choices are the same rounded-rectangle button as Add expense / Income / Transfer, with the picked one dark; the period is Home's calendar pill (icon, the period with "Week 1 of 5" under it, a chevron) between the same arrows; Home's ring legend is capped at 200 dp and centred beside the ring so a name and its figure stay close; and the Insights sheet uses the non-scrolling sheet layout (it scrolls only above 1.2 font scale or on a screen under 700 dp), which should sit it flush at the bottom. That last one is untested on a device.
- The entries of each day sit on a card (surface, hairline, 18 dp corner, 14 dp side padding, the same as Home's list cards) so amounts read on a solid ground instead of straight on the wallpaper. The day heading and the marker rail stay outside it.
- Edge alignment from a phone screenshot: the period row's back arrow and the Week | Month switch carried 10 dp of touch padding, so the arrow sat 10 dp inside the left gutter and the word "Month" stopped 10 dp short of the cards' right edge. The row and the switch are pulled out by that padding, so both sit on the 16 dp gutter with the touch areas unchanged. The stacked layout (below 380 dp or above 1.15 font scale) is not offset.

## Review of the whole screen (2026-11-12)

A review of what a person sees, how it moves, and what they would think, done from the code (the screen has not been seen on a device since). Each item below was a fault, not a taste; the design is unchanged.

- **The loading placeholder was the old page.** It drew the tall Spent card with seven bars and one big day card, so every cold start reflowed into the slim strip, the filter row and the days with their rail. [TransactionsSkeleton](../src/features/transactions/TransactionsSkeleton.tsx) is now drawn from the real measures (the 64 dp strip, the 40 dp filter row, days with the 44 dp rail and a card of entries).
- **The page turned in two halves.** On a period step the strip glided in while the entries snapped. The days that land with a period now fade in with it (`CONTENT_ENTER`, for 400 ms after the data lands, through the days' `entering` prop), and the same fade replaces the one-frame swap from the placeholder. Days that scroll into view later mount without it, so scrolling a long month never flickers; reduce-motion turns it off like every other motion.
- **A past week said "This week".** The bars' caption under the strip now says what the bars are ("By day", "By week"), and an empty past period names itself ("Nothing logged in 3–9 Nov", "…in October 2025") instead of "Nothing logged this week" ([emptyPeriodTitle](../src/features/transactions/transactions.helpers.ts)).
- **Search lost the year.** Search spans the whole ledger, and the feed redesign had dropped the visible date line, so "11 Nov" in two different years looked the same. A result's heading now names the year unless it is this year ([searchDayLabel](../src/features/transactions/transactions.helpers.ts)).
- **Search stopped at 50 without saying so.** The query now asks for one more than the list holds; the list shows how many matched ("12 matches") and, when more did, "Newest 50 matches. Add a word to narrow it." The count is announced to a screen reader as it changes. Scrolling the results also puts the keyboard away (`keyboardDismissMode`), so the lower entries are not hidden behind it.
- **The Label filter sat outside the filter row.** It was a lone chip on its own line, not counted as picked, so Clear all did not appear for it alone. It is now a chip in the same row as the categories and accounts, and counts toward Clear all.

Left as is, on purpose: Insights' bars still grow from empty each time the sheet opens (staggered, 420 ms), which the motion rules elsewhere say not to do on first view; it is the payoff of the tap and was kept. The picker's "This month" button returns to the current period of whichever scope is on (this week in Week), so its label is not exact in Week. A shortcut back to the current week once several have been stepped away is a feature, not a fix, and is not added.
