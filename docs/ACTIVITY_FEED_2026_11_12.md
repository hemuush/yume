# Activity: feed first (2026-11-12)

Activity now leads with its entries. The summary is one slim strip, the filters are one row, and the days are a flat dated feed. Calculations, privacy rules, period loading, search and hand-ordering are unchanged; this is presentation.

## What changed

- **Spent strip** ([ActivitySummaryStrip](../src/features/transactions/ActivitySummaryStrip.tsx)) replaces the big Spent card. It shows the kicker ("Spent this week", "Spent on Food this week", or just "Spent" for a past period), the figure, the change against the period before (dropped while the list is narrowed to a category) and a small bar per day (per week in a month) with the current day in ink. It is a button that opens Insights. The figure is a plain amount that never counts up; the strip as a whole glides in when the period steps (see below).
- **Insights sheet** holds what the card used to: the figure and change, the full day chart (tap a day to see only it, which closes the sheet and scrolls the list), the daily average, and Money in / Left. It is the same [TransactionsHeadline](../src/features/transactions/TransactionsHeadline.tsx) with `bare`, so the chart, scaling and privacy logic are not duplicated.
- **One filter row** ([ActivityFilterChips](../src/features/transactions/ActivityFilterChips.tsx)): the Filters button (with its count), then the picked category, account and label chips, then All / Spent / Income / Transfers, and Clear all last (see the device note below). It scrolls sideways, and wraps at 360 dp or above 1.2 font scale. The second chip line is gone.
- **Flat dated feed** ([TimelineDay](../src/features/transactions/TimelineDay.tsx)): each day is a block on the page, not a segment of one card. A marker rail on the left shows the weekday and date number (today's in a filled circle) and a line down to the next day, which the last day ends. The day's heading names what it did instead of one unlabelled net: "₹1,420 spent · ₹82,000 in · ₹899 back" (refunds are counted as "back", not as income, and transfers move nothing). It is omitted when one line would only repeat it, and savings amounts are left out while hidden, as before.

Unchanged: the Week | Month switch and the date pill stay in the header, the entry rows (a note leads with the category, parent and account under it), stacks, drag ordering, search, the filter sheet and the detail sheet.

## Checks

## After the first look on a phone

- The "avg ₹… a week" name on the dashed line was drawn before the capsules, so a capsule painted over part of it in the wider Insights sheet. The line still runs behind the capsules; its name is now drawn after them ([SpendBarChart](../src/features/transactions/SpendBarChart.tsx)).
- The picked day's amount bubble was cut off ("₹3,710" read "3,7…" with a flat top): it was drawn inside the capsule, which clips its children to the capsule's 26 dp width and 52 dp height. It is now drawn last, on a row laid out like the columns ([SpendBarChart](../src/features/transactions/SpendBarChart.tsx)), so it is whole, sits over the neighbouring capsules, and the room above the bars grows with the text size. Where it would land on the average's name (a picked day among the last three columns, at the line's height) the name steps aside until another day is picked; the line stays. "The last three columns" is a place, not a measured overlap of the words.
- Opening or closing a group ("Home 2 ⌄") popped its entries in and out and made the days below jump. A first fix faded the entries and slid the day with a layout animation, but the list's own cell changes height at once, so the days below still jumped (a gap opened and filled, or the days slid up over the fading entries). The entries now sit in a [Collapsible](../src/components/Collapsible.tsx): it measures them, then animates its own height and opacity from a shared value (`useAnimatedStyle`, the path the progress bars use, 200 ms on the shared curve), so the cell and every day below move with it, frame by frame. In the browser render with a frame-by-frame stand-in the next day's top went 624 → 714 dp over about 200 ms, and back on closing, with no step; taps in quick succession, reduce-motion and scrolling away and back all end in the right state. It is taken out of the tree once closed and starts from nothing when opened again. A group that is already open when it scrolls into view is simply open. If the measuring ever fails the entries still show at full height (they just do not glide), and if the animation does not take, a watchdog shows them as plain rows. The group's own line and the day carry no layout animation. Reduce-motion makes the panel jump. A first attempt used a CSS height transition and did not work on a phone (see the review); this one uses the engine the rest of the app's bars use. Not run on a phone. Which stacks are open is held in a small store, not in screen state, so a tap re-renders only that line, and a finger touching the line prepares its panel so the tap only starts the glide (see the review, thirteenth pass).
- The Spent strip lost the glide the old card made when the period stepped. [usePeriodSlide](../src/features/transactions/usePeriodSlide.ts) is that glide as one hook (18 dp from the side it came from with a fade, a plain fade for a picked period, nothing under reduce-motion); the strip and the Insights figures both use it.
- In the filter row only the four type choices form the radio group. The Filters button and the removable chips were inside it, which read wrongly to a screen reader.
- Small consistency tweaks with Home, no layout change: the type choices are the same rounded-rectangle button as Add expense / Income / Transfer, with the picked one dark; the period is Home's calendar pill (icon, the period with "Week 1 of 5" under it, a chevron) between the same arrows; Home's ring legend is capped at 200 dp and centred beside the ring so a name and its figure stay close; and the Insights sheet uses the non-scrolling sheet layout (it scrolls only above 1.2 font scale or on a screen under 700 dp), which should sit it flush at the bottom. That last one is untested on a device.
- The entries of each day sit on a card (surface, hairline, 18 dp corner, 14 dp side padding, the same as Home's list cards) so amounts read on a solid ground instead of straight on the wallpaper. The day heading and the marker rail stay outside it.
- Edge alignment from a phone screenshot: the period row's back arrow and the Week | Month switch carried 10 dp of touch padding, so the arrow sat 10 dp inside the left gutter and the word "Month" stopped 10 dp short of the cards' right edge. The row and the switch are pulled out by that padding, so both sit on the 16 dp gutter with the touch areas unchanged. The stacked layout (below 380 dp or above 1.15 font scale) is not offset.

## Review of the whole screen (2026-11-12)

A review of what a person sees, how it moves, and what they would think, done from the code (the screen has not been seen on a device since). Each item below was a fault, not a taste; the design is unchanged.

- **The loading placeholder was the old page.** It drew the tall Spent card with seven bars and one big day card, so every cold start reflowed into the slim strip, the filter row and the days with their rail. [TransactionsSkeleton](../src/features/transactions/TransactionsSkeleton.tsx) is now drawn from the real measures (the 64 dp strip, the 40 dp filter row, days with the 44 dp rail and a card of entries).
- **The page turned in two halves.** On a period step the strip glided in while the entries snapped. The days that land with a period now fade in with it (`CONTENT_ENTER`, for 400 ms after the data lands, through the days' `entering` prop), and the same fade replaces the one-frame swap from the placeholder. Days that scroll into view later mount without it, so scrolling a long month never flickers; reduce-motion turns it off like every other motion.
- **A past week said "This week".** The bars' caption under the strip now says what the bars are ("By day", "By week"), and an empty past period names itself ("Nothing logged in 3–9 Nov", "…in October 2025") instead of "Nothing logged this week" ([emptyPeriodTitle](../src/features/transactions/transactions.helpers.ts)).
- **Search lost the year.** Search spans the whole ledger, and the feed redesign had dropped the visible date line, so "11 Nov" in two different years looked the same. A result's heading now names the year unless it is this year ([searchDayLabel](../src/features/transactions/transactions.helpers.ts)).
- **Search stopped at 50 without saying so.** The query now asks for one more than the list holds; the list shows how many matched ("12 matches") and, when more did, "Newest 50 matches. Add a word to narrow it." The count is announced to a screen reader as it changes. Scrolling the results also puts the keyboard away (`keyboardDismissMode`), so the lower entries are not hidden behind it.
- **The Label filter sat outside the filter row.** It was a lone chip on its own line, not counted as picked, so Clear all did not appear for it alone. It is now a chip in the same row as the categories and accounts, and counts toward Clear all.

Left as is, on purpose: Insights' bars still grow from empty each time the sheet opens (staggered, 420 ms), which the motion rules elsewhere say not to do on first view; it is the payoff of the tap and was kept. The picker's "This month" button returns to the current period of whichever scope is on (this week in Week), so its label is not exact in Week. A shortcut back to the current week once several have been stepped away is a feature, not a fix, and is not added.

Checks: `TransactionsSkeleton.test.tsx`, `SearchStatus.test.tsx` (count, newest-50 note, live region), `TransactionsScreen.test.tsx` (the 51-row ask, the year, the past-period title, the days' fade, the label chip) and `transactions.helpers.test.ts`. Unit tests only: the fade and the skeleton's fit are unconfirmed until seen on a phone.

## Second review: the sheets, search and the gestures (2026-11-12)

The same kind of review for what the first one did not cover: the filter sheet, the entry sheet, the back button, scrolling and reordering. Again each item was a fault.

- **A new period opened wherever the last one was scrolled to.** A list keeps its offset when its data is replaced, so picking a month from the shrunk header landed mid-way down it (or at its end) with the Spent strip out of view. [useScrollToTopOnChange](../src/lib/useScrollToTopOnChange.ts) returns the list to its top when the period shown changes, and leaves it alone when the same period reloads (after an edit, say).
- **Back left the tab with search open.** On Android the back button or gesture now leaves search, as Cancel does (a listener that exists only while search is open and the tab is in view). Only onboarding handled back before.
- **The filter sheet's label box corrected and capitalised what was typed**, though a label matches exactly as written ("Lunch" and "lunch" are two labels everywhere in the app). It now turns both off, and says "Tap a label below to filter by it." while a half-typed word narrows the list, so it no longer applies as "Show 0 entries". `searchInputs.test.ts` now finds search boxes by their placeholder as well as their accessibility label, which is how this one had been missed; it also found Add's "Find or create a label", which stops autocorrecting (it still capitalises, as it makes new names).
- **An old entry's date had no year.** The entry sheet read "Thu 1 Oct" for an entry from any year, and search or a picked month can open one from years back. The year now shows unless it is this year ([entryDateLabel](../src/features/transactions/transactions.helpers.ts)).
- **Dropping a reordered line made no feel.** Lifting and each place passed over tick; landing in a new place now confirms with the usual completion haptic. Letting go where it started stays silent.

Looked at and left: Spent / Expense and Transfers / Transfer are different words in the filter row and the filter sheet (the row follows the Spent strip, the sheet follows Add); the bar chart's day totals are floored at zero when a refund exceeds the day's spending, so a week's bars can add to a little more than the strip's figure; a swipe on the Spent strip needs 60 dp of travel and ignores speed.

Checks: `useScrollToTopOnChange.test.tsx`, the back-button test in `TransactionsScreen.test.tsx`, the label box tests in `FilterModal.test.tsx`, the old-entry year in `TransactionDetailModal.test.tsx`, the haptic on drop in `TimelineDay.test.tsx`, and the widened `searchInputs.test.ts`. As before these are unit tests: the screen has not been run on a device since.

## Seen on a device: Clear all came first (2026-11-13)

The first look at Activity on a phone showed the filter row as Filters, Clear all, All, Spent, Income, Transfers. A picked type counts as a filter, so tapping Spent made Clear all appear in front of the type choices and moved them to the right under the thumb that had just tapped one. It now comes last, after the types, in the scrolling row and in the wrapped row at large text: the types never move. The chips picked in the filter sheet were first left in front of the types, beside the Filters button; with eight picked they pushed All, Spent, Income and Transfers off the screen (seen on a device), so everything picked now follows the types, and Clear all ends the row. It still appears for a type on its own, as before, although All does the same there; that, and whether it should wait until two filters are on, is a choice left open. [ActivityFilterChips.test.tsx](../src/features/transactions/ActivityFilterChips.test.tsx) reads the row's tappable things in order in both layouts.

## Rendered on a real database (2026-11-13)

Activity was rendered from the real screen in a browser on a real database (the app's own db layer on SQLite compiled to JavaScript, built with the app's own write functions: 75 days of spending, groups of entries in one category on one day, a transfer, a long-noted income, a refund), with the real fonts, at 360, 390 and 412 dp, in week and month scope, with a group expanded, with search open, a past week and with no data. Positions were measured and every cut-off text and small target was listed by script. The harness lives outside the repository and approximates Android; the filter sheet and period picker need native pieces it lacks and were not rendered.

- **The filter row, and the list under it, dropped 2 dp when "Clear all" arrived.** Clear all and a picked chip are 44 dp tall and the pills 40, so the row grew by 4 dp when one of them appeared and centred the pills lower. The row is 44 dp tall from the start; picking a type moves nothing (measured: every pill and the first entry keep their position).
- **Picking a type changed the width of the pills** (the picked one went from regular to medium), moving its neighbours by up to 1 dp. All pills use one weight.
- **Switching Week | Month moved the words** by about 2.5 dp (the picked one was bold, and the row is right-aligned). Both are one weight; the underline and the darker ink show the pick.
- **A transfer's title stranded its last word** ("SBI → Emergency / fund"). The arrow stays with where it goes, so it wraps as "SBI →" over "Emergency fund".

Nothing is cut off at 360, 390 or 412 dp. Expanding a group leaves its own row where it is (measured). The figures agree: the strip's total is the days' spending less the refund, and month and Reports agree.

### Looked at and left

- At 360 dp the period bar stacks (the pill on one line, Week | Month on a second line of two equal halves with a long underline). That is the designed narrow layout (`stackPeriod`, under 380 dp); on one line the pill and the switch do not fit at 360.
- The previous and next week arrows are 36 dp wide (40 with their slop) and 44 tall: a little under 44 across.
- The week's label is built as "8–" plus a day and month, so a phone set to a US locale reads "8–Nov 14" where an Indian one reads "8–14 Nov". Fine for the app's audience; the locale's own range format would settle it.
- A day's heading wraps its totals under the name when they are long ("₹5,625 spent · ₹450 back"); it is left-aligned and one line taller, by design.
