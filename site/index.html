A little ring that keeps you company

Suu is the ring from Yume's logo, with one coral dot to give it a personality. Suu is not a chatbot and never goes online: everything it says is worked out from your own numbers, on your phone.

Your money stays yours

Privacy is how Yume is built, not a setting you have to find.

On your phone

Records live in a database on your device. There is no Yume account or server.

No tracking

No analytics and no ads. Release builds do not ask for Internet access at all.

You choose backups

Backups and exports go only where you send them, such as a folder you pick.

Lock and hide

Use your fingerprint or PIN, and hide amounts on screen and in widgets.

Install in four steps

  1. Open the download on your phone.
  2. If Android asks, allow your browser or Files app to install apps.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, choose Install anyway: Yume is not on the Play Store yet.
  4. Open Yume, then go to Settings → Notifications and tap Allow so reminders arrive on time.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. The code is open on GitHub under its licence.

Does it work on iPhone?

No. Yume is built for Android.

Do I need internet?

No. It works fully offline.

What if I lose my phone?

Your records are only on the phone, so keep a backup copy somewhere you trust. Yume can save backups to a folder you choose.

What can Yume access on my phone?

Only what it needs: fingerprint or face unlock for the app lock, notifications and exact alarms for reminders, and vibration. It has no Internet permission and cannot reach your contacts, messages, location, camera or microphone.

How do I back up, or move to a new phone?

Save a backup file yourself, or pick a folder and Yume saves backups there while it is open. On the new phone, restore from that file or folder. You see what is in the backup and what is on the phone before anything is replaced.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Make a PDF report or an Excel workbook for a month, a year or everything. It is made on your phone and handed to Android's share sheet, so it goes only where you send it.

Why does Android warn me when I install?

Yume is not on the Play Store yet, so Android shows its standard warning for apps installed from a file. Compare the checksum above with your download if you want to be sure it is the same file.

My reminders arrive late.

On newer Android, allow Alarms & reminders for Yume. Open Settings → Notifications in the app and tap Allow.

How do I run it myself? 
git clone https://github.com/hemuush/yume.git
cd yume
npm ci
npx expo run:android
