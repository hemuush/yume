Yume

Privacy

Yume is a money tracker that works on your phone. This page says what it does with your data.

What is stored

The entries, accounts, loans, goals and settings you add are kept in a database inside the app on your phone. They are not sent to the developer or to any server.

What is not collected

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • No bank logins or card details.
  • Release builds do not request Internet permission.

Backups and exports

You can save a backup, a PDF report or an Excel file. These leave the app only to the place you choose through Android's share sheet or a folder you pick. Those files are readable and may hold sensitive details, so keep them somewhere safe. The app or cloud service you send them to controls its own copy.

Locking and hiding

App lock uses your phone's fingerprint, face or PIN. You can also hide amounts in the app and, separately, in home screen widgets. App lock does not encrypt the database or your exports.

Notifications

Reminders are created on your phone. On newer Android versions you may need to allow Alarms & reminders for them to arrive on time.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Yume or clearing its data removes the database from your phone. Copies you exported or backed up stay where you saved them until you delete them.

Questions

Open an issue on GitHub. Please do not attach personal ledgers or backups.
